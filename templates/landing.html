Security is built in, not bolted on

CoreCloud ships with its own Security Operations Center — the same kind of tooling large companies use, running inside your portal.

  • Full audit trail — every login, change and export is logged.
  • Threat detection — brute force, credential stuffing, suspicious IPs and more.
  • Auto-response — suspicious accounts are locked and sessions revoked automatically.
  • Account controls — IT can blacklist/whitelist users and IPs instantly.
  • Hardened sign-in — lockout after failed attempts, HTTPS-only cookies, face login.
