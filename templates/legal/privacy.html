Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who We Are

CoreCloud is a company portal operated for its subscribing organizations. For data protection purposes, your organization (the employer) is the controller of the employee data entered into the Service, and CoreCloud acts as its processor.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account & profile data — name, employee ID, email address, role, department and profile picture.
  • HR & payroll data — attendance, leave records, salary and payroll information as configured by your organization.
  • Security data — login events, IP addresses, device/user-agent information and audit trail entries used for threat detection.
  • Face templates — if you register for face login, a mathematical representation of your face features is stored to enable recognition. Face images used for registration are processed to produce this template.

3. How We Use Data

  • To provide the portal's HR, payroll and collaboration features to your organization.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To detect, investigate and prevent security incidents (SOC/SIEM functionality).
  • To send service notifications (e.g. leave decisions, password resets) as configured by your organization.

4. Security

We protect data in transit with HTTPS, restrict access to authorized users, maintain an audit trail of sensitive actions, and apply automated security controls (lockouts, session revocation, IP/user blacklisting) to detect abuse.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is accessible to your organization's administrators and is processed by hosting and infrastructure providers (application hosting and database services) strictly to run the Service.

6. Retention

Data is retained for as long as your organization maintains its account, or as required by applicable law. Administrators can request deletion of employee records through the portal's administration functions.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal data. Requests should be directed to your organization's administrator, who controls the data in CoreCloud.

8. Face Login Data

Face templates are used only for authentication. You may request removal of your face registration at any time through the face management screen or by contacting your administrator, and can use password login instead.

9. Cookies

The Service uses strictly necessary session and CSRF cookies to keep you signed in and protect against cross-site request forgery. These are not used for advertising.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the Service.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact your organization's administrator or the support channels provided with the Service.
